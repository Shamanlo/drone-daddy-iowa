Service · Events

Events

Venues, festivals, and gatherings captured from above — planned properly, flown legally.

The problem

Scale is the story, and scale needs height.

The photograph that shows how big the turnout was, how the grounds were laid out, or what the venue looks like full — that shot only exists from the air. It's the frame that ends up on next year's promotional material.

What you get
  • PlanningAirspace checked, flight windows agreed with organisers, and operations planned around where people will be.
  • CoverageEstablishing shots of the grounds and venue, plus aerial video for recap and promotional use.
  • DeliverablesEdited stills and a short cut, with vertical versions for social.
  • Turnaround48 hours standard. Same-day highlights available by arrangement.
Worth knowing

Flying over crowds is regulated, and some sites and dates require additional authorisation or advance coordination. Booking early gives time to plan the operation properly rather than working around restrictions on the day.

Send the venue, date, and expected attendance. Early enquiries are easier to plan and cheaper to fly.
