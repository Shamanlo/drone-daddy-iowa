Service · Construction

Construction & job site progress

Repeatable aerial documentation of your build, from the same angles, on a schedule you set.

The problem

A job site looks different from 200 feet.

Ground photos show a corner of the work. An aerial shows the whole site — grading, staging, sequencing, and how much has actually changed since last month. Owners understand it immediately, lenders accept it, and when a dispute comes up two years later, you have a dated record instead of an argument.

What you get
  • ConsistencyThe same flight path and the same angles every visit, so month-to-month comparison is honest and immediate.
  • CadenceMonthly, per-milestone, or on call. Most builds run monthly with extra visits at foundation, dry-in, and completion.
  • DeliverablesDated stills at full resolution plus a short overview video. Unedited by default — progress documentation should be accurate, not graded.
  • Turnaround48 hours standard. Rush available when a draw request is waiting.
Who books this

General contractors, developers, project managers, and owners' reps running commercial, multifamily, and large residential builds anywhere in the state.
