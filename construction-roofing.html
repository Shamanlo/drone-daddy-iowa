What We Shoot

Aerial Work For Builders, Roofers & Property Owners

Construction drone photography covers more than a flyover. Here's what's included when you book this service.

  • Roofing Inspections & Documentation

    Overhead and angled shots of a roof's condition before work starts, and clean documentation once it's done. Useful for estimates, insurance claims, and proof of workmanship.

  • Before & After Transformations

    Matched aerial angles shot before and after a project, so the change is obvious at a glance. Works for roofs, additions, landscaping, and full property renovations.

  • Building & Job Site Progression

    Return visits at key milestones to track a build from foundation to framing to finish. Gives you a dated visual record of progress over the life of the project.

  • Construction Site Overviews

    Wide aerial shots that show the whole site, the surrounding lot, and how the project fits into its setting, useful for planning, permitting, and marketing.

  • Editing For Social Media & Web

    Footage cut and optimized for how you'll actually use it: vertical reels for Instagram and TikTok, and web-ready clips sized for your site.

  • Insurance & Documentation Support

    Clear, dated aerial evidence that contractors, adjusters, and clients can all reference from the same set of images.

Every project ships edited. Color, cuts, and captions where they help, sized for wherever the footage will live.
