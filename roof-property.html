Roof & property documentation

Fast visual condition imagery for contractors and inspectors. No ladder, no half day.

The problem

Sending someone up a ladder costs more than this does.

Every lead that needs a look at the roof costs a crew member, a drive, and an hour of liability. Aerial imagery gets you high-resolution coverage of every slope and penetration in a fraction of the time, and gives you something to show the property owner on the spot.

What you get
  • CoverageFull-resolution imagery of every roof plane, plus overhead orientation shots and detail passes on areas you flag.
  • TurnaroundSame-day or next-day delivery, because these usually sit inside a sales cycle.
  • FormatOrganised, labelled image sets ready to drop into your own report or estimate.
  • ScopeImagery and documentation only. Condition assessment, measurement, and any professional opinion stay with you.
Who books this

Roofing contractors, property managers, and home inspectors who want visual documentation faster and cheaper than putting a person on the roof.
