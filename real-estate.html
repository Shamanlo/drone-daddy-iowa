Real estate & land

Listings, acreage, and commercial parcels shown the way a buyer needs to see them.

The problem

Some properties can't be photographed from the ground.

Acreage, farmland, corner lots, commercial buildings, and development parcels all have the same issue — the thing that makes them valuable is the land itself, and you cannot show land from a driveway. Aerial imagery shows boundaries, access, surrounding context, and scale in one frame.

What you get
  • StillsEdited, full-resolution aerial photographs sized for MLS, print, and brochures.
  • VideoA short cut for the listing page, plus a vertical version for Instagram and Facebook.
  • Context shotsElevation and boundary perspective — where the parcel sits relative to roads, water, treelines, and neighbouring development.
  • Turnaround48 hours standard, so the listing goes live on schedule.
Who books this

Land brokers, commercial brokers, and agents listing acreage, estate properties, and anything where the lot is the selling point. Aerial only — I don't shoot interiors, and I'm happy to work alongside your existing photographer.
