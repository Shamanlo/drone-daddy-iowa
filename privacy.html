Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Drone Daddy Iowa ("we," "us") operates dronedaddyiowa.com. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you submit our quote form, we receive the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address, the service you're interested in, and your message. Form submissions are processed by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, which transmits your message to our email.
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used for security and to keep the site running.
  • Embedded video. Pages on this site embed videos hosted on YouTube (via YouTube's privacy-enhanced player). When you play a video, YouTube may collect data in accordance with Google's privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and communicate with you about your project.
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Aerial photography and video

Our work involves capturing aerial photos and video of properties and job sites at the request of our clients. We do not intentionally capture identifiable images of people who are not part of a project, and we fly in accordance with FAA Part 107 regulations. If you believe you appear in footage on this site and would like it addressed, contact us using the information below.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to run this website and deliver your messages (such as our hosting provider and Web3Forms), or if required by law.

Data retention

We keep quote inquiries as long as needed to respond to you and manage our client relationships, then delete them when no longer needed.

Your choices

  • You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you or to request that it be corrected or deleted.
  • You can choose not to play embedded videos if you prefer that YouTube not receive data about your visit.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version on this page with a revised date.
