Pre-Flight Questions

FAQ

Are you FAA licensed?

Yes. Drone Daddy Iowa holds an FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate, the federal license required to fly drones commercially in the United States. Every flight follows Part 107 rules, including airspace authorization when required.

Can you fly anywhere?

Most places, yes. Airspace near airports, certain events, and some public lands needs prior FAA authorization, and a few areas are off limits entirely. We check airspace for every job before we quote it. In controlled airspace we can usually get authorization quickly through the FAA's LAANC system.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry liability insurance for our drone operations and can provide a certificate of insurance on request. Many contractors and venues require one before work begins.

Where are you based and what cities do you serve?

We're based in North Liberty, Iowa and serve the surrounding corridor: Iowa City, Coralville, Cedar Rapids, Solon, and communities across Eastern Iowa. For larger projects we travel statewide.

Why choose Drone Daddy Iowa?

We're local, licensed, and insured, and we deliver edited footage you can actually use, not just raw files. We shoot for roofers, builders, realtors, and homeowners across Eastern Iowa, and we check airspace before every flight.

Can you edit the videos?

Yes. Editing is part of the job: color, music, titles, and cuts sized for wherever the footage will live, from YouTube to your website to social media reels. If you have your own editor, we can deliver raw footage instead.
